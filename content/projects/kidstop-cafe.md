# KidStop Cafe: Family Dining Website

## Introduction

A modern website for KidStop Cafe, a family-friendly cafe in Karamürsel built around the concept "You Coffee, They Play." The cafe offers parents a peaceful coffee experience while children enjoy a dedicated play area. The website provides an online menu, contact information, and ordering capabilities -- bringing the cafe's warm atmosphere to the digital space.

## Key Features

- **Online Menu**: Digital menu display with categories, descriptions, and pricing
- **Contact & Location**: Easy access to contact details, location information, and business hours
- **Responsive Design**: Optimized for mobile, tablet, and desktop -- customers can browse the menu on their phones while visiting
- **Brand Identity**: Clean design reflecting the family-friendly, welcoming atmosphere of the physical cafe

## Technologies Used

Built as a responsive web application with modern frontend technologies, deployed to kidstopcafe.com.
