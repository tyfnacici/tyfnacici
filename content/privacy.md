## Who this applies to

Hermes AI Assistant is a private, self-hosted personal assistant. It is used only by its owner, Tayfun Açıcı. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

## Data the app accesses

With the owner's consent, Hermes AI Assistant requests access to the owner's own Google account data: Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Business Profile.

## How the data is used

The data is used only to carry out tasks that the owner requests. It is processed on the owner's own computer.

## Sharing

The data is not sold, not shared with third parties and not used for advertising. It is not used to train models of a third party.

## Google API Services User Data Policy

Hermes AI Assistant's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Retention and deletion

Data stays until the owner deletes it. Access can be revoked at any time at [https://myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
