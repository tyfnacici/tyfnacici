## Overview

I design and deploy in-house AI solutions that automate real business workflows — not generic chatbots, but purpose-built systems that connect your internal data, compliance rules, and operational processes with large language models (LLMs). My focus is on practical, measurable automation that saves time and reduces manual effort.

## What I Offer

### Custom AI Workflows
- **LLM-powered pipelines**: Connect Claude, GPT, or open-source models to your internal databases and APIs
- **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)**: Question-answering systems over your company documentation, policies, and knowledge bases
- **Compliance guardrails**: AI responses bounded by your internal rules and regulatory requirements

### Agentic Systems
- **Multi-agent orchestration**: Hierarchies of specialized AI agents — one to retrieve data, another to analyze, another to format output
- **Sub-agent delegation**: Complex tasks broken down into parallel sub-tasks handled by independent AI agents
- **Human-in-the-loop**: Critical decisions routed to human approvers before execution

### Business Process Automation
- **Document processing**: Auto-extract, classify, and route invoices, contracts, and forms
- **Reporting automation**: AI-generated daily/weekly reports from structured and unstructured data
- **Workflow triggers**: Event-driven automation (new order → AI checks inventory → generates response)

## Technologies

**LLMs:** Claude API, OpenAI API, open-source models
**Frameworks:** LangChain, custom agent orchestration
**Data:** RAG pipelines, vector databases, structured SQL
**Integration:** REST APIs, webhooks, message queues
**Deployment:** Docker, serverless (AWS Lambda)
