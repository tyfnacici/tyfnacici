## Overview

I build custom WordPress solutions - from boutique WooCommerce stores to enterprise e-commerce platforms integrated with ERP systems. With hands-on production experience building WooCommerce extensions with real-time ERP sync, I understand the full ecosystem: themes, plugins, REST APIs, and third-party integrations.

### WooCommerce Stores
- **Custom store setups**: Product catalogs, payment gateways, shipping configurations, tax rules
- **Bulk operations**: High-volume product import/export, batch price updates, stock synchronization
- **ERP integration**: Real-time sync between WooCommerce and your ERP system via REST API - proven with live MSSQL bridge implementations
- **Rate limiting and logging**: Production-ready plugins with throttling and operational visibility

### WordPress Development
- **Custom themes**: Bespoke designs tailored to your brand - responsive, performant, maintainable
- **Plugin development**: Custom functionality extending WordPress core without bloat
- **REST API extensions**: Expose custom endpoints for headless WordPress or third-party integrations
